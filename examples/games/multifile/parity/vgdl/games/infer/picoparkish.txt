BasicGame
    SpriteSet
        floor > Immovable img=colors/LIGHTGRAY
        wall > Immovable img=colors/DARKGRAY
        breakwall > Immovable img=colors/BROWN
        button > Immovable img=colors/YELLOW
        exit > Immovable img=colors/GREEN
        avatar > GravityAvatar img=colors/RED

    LevelMapping
        . > floor
        # > floor wall
        W > floor breakwall
        B > floor button
        D > floor exit
        A > floor avatar

    TerminationSet
        SpriteCounter stype=avatar limit=0 win=False

    InteractionSet
        avatar wall > stepBack
        avatar breakwall > stepBack
        
        avatar button > DestroyAllBreakwalls
        avatar exit > killSprite scoreChange=100
        
        avatar EOS > stepBack
        wall EOS > killSprite
        breakwall EOS > killSprite
        button EOS > killSprite
        exit EOS > killSprite
