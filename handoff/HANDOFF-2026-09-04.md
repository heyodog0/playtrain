## 4. Open decisions (all Ryan's)

1. **Panel A design:** two-arm (as-shipped + tuned) vs tuned-only. The 09-01
   decision was two arms; the best-only render is cleaner and keeps the
   crossover legible, but moves the as-shipped contrast into caption prose.
2. **Legend wording:** prefer "EnvPool (tuned)" over "documented best" —
   EnvPool publishes no ProcGen benchmark at all, so the provenance claim
   belongs in the caption, not the legend.
3. **qbert / aim_trainer:** published learning curves predate the style-cache
   determinism fix. Footnote, or retrain (minutes).
4. **flappy_bird / vvvvvv** wall-clock configs are not in the suite3 manifest
   — locate them if those curves must also reflect adv.
5. **Build policy:** portable (no PGO, ~+21%) vs tuned (adv, needs
   `wt/pgo/*.profdata`) as the released artifact. Interacts with the
   build-provenance asymmetry: adv is a PGO/-march binary while EnvPool runs
   from its generic manylinux wheel. Options are headline-the-portable-build,
   source-build EnvPool (half-day Bazel), or a shipped-vs-shipped caption
   clause.
