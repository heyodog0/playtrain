## 3. ICNN topology sweep — keep 12w / 2+2 (jobs 44806296, 44829687)

| split | workers | envs | steps/s | vs 12w/2+2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2+2 | 8 | 4,096 | 360,441 | 1.019 |
| 2+2 | **12** | **6,144** | **352,238** | 1.000 |
| 2+2 | 15 | 7,680 | 356,059 | 1.011 |
| 2+2 | 16 | 8,192 | 357,119 | 1.014 |
| 1+3 | any | any | ~192,000 | 0.55 |
| 3+1 | 12 | 6,144 | 217,897 | 0.62 |

**The GPU split is the whole story; worker count is noise** (3.8% spread over a
2x change in workers; best beats 12w by 1.9%, inside the noise floor). A second
learner GPU is worth 1.83x of one, and a single inference GPU caps the row at
~218k regardless of learner. Recommendation taken: keep 12w/2+2, matches
`tab:hyperparams`, no 24-game re-measure.

Nice appendix-worthy finding: **the row does not need 6,144 environments in
flight, it needs a second learner GPU** — 8 workers with 4,096 envs matches 12
with 6,144.
