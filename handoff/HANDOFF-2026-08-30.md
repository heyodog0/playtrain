Interpretation: the ~450k/s ProcGen ceiling appears on BOTH architectures

PongNoFrameskip on a 256-core DGX in **async+NUMA mode = 8 NUMA-bound pools**,
i.e. their own headline requires sharding the pool. Per-core they get ~4.2k
steps/s; our Pong measures ~3.7k/thread — per-core parity, no contradiction.
